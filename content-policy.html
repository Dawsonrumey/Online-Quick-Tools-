Important: This policy explains what users may and may not submit to Online Quick Tools through our video, image, community and other user-content features.

1. Purpose of This Policy

Online Quick Tools allows users to interact with certain community features, including submitting images, videos, links and other content.

This Community & Content Policy helps keep the platform useful, safe, respectful and suitable for a broad audience.

This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2. User Responsibility

You are responsible for anything you submit, upload, publish, or share through Online Quick Tools.

Before submitting content, make sure that you have the necessary rights, permissions and authority to share it.

3. Allowed Content

Generally allowed:
  • Original photos, images and artwork
  • Original videos
  • Educational and informational content
  • Helpful tutorials and demonstrations
  • Creative and entertainment content
  • Family-friendly community content
  • Content that you have permission to publish
  • Links to legitimate websites and platforms

4. Prohibited Content

Users must not submit or distribute:
  • Illegal content
  • Content that promotes criminal activity
  • Malware, viruses or harmful files
  • Spam or repeated unwanted submissions
  • Scams or fraudulent content
  • Phishing links or deceptive websites
  • Content designed to steal passwords or personal information
  • Impersonation or misleading identity claims
  • Content that infringes copyright or trademarks
  • Content that violates another person's privacy
  • Non-consensual private or intimate material
  • Threats, harassment or targeted abuse
  • Hateful or discriminatory content
  • Content encouraging dangerous or harmful activities
  • Content involving exploitation or abuse of minors
  • Content that is sexually explicit
  • Extremely graphic violent content
  • Fraudulent advertisements or misleading promotions

5. Copyright

You should only upload or publish content that you created yourself or that you have permission to use.

Do not upload copyrighted videos, images, music, articles or other material unless you have the necessary rights or permission.

If a valid copyright complaint is received, the relevant content may be restricted or removed.

6. User-Submitted Videos

Users may be allowed to submit videos or links to supported platforms and services.

Submitting a video does not guarantee that it will be published.

Videos may be reviewed before publication and may be rejected or removed if they violate this policy, our Terms & Conditions, or applicable law.

7. User-Submitted Images

Users may be allowed to upload images through our Image Hub or other supported features.

Users must ensure that uploaded images comply with this policy.

Images may be reviewed, restricted or removed when necessary.

8. Privacy and Personal Information

Do not publish another person's private information without appropriate permission.

This includes information such as:

  • Private phone numbers
  • Private email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Authentication codes
  • Private addresses
  • Financial account information
  • Other sensitive personal information

Users should also avoid publicly posting their own sensitive information.

9. Spam and Promotional Content

Spam is not allowed.

Examples include:

  • Repeatedly posting the same content
  • Posting unrelated promotional messages
  • Mass posting links
  • Fake engagement
  • Deceptive referral promotions
  • Automated submissions intended to abuse the platform

10. External Links

Users may submit links where the relevant feature allows it.

Links must not intentionally direct users to malware, phishing pages, scams or other harmful destinations.

Online Quick Tools does not guarantee the safety, accuracy or availability of third-party websites.

11. Advertising and Monetization

Online Quick Tools may use advertising or other legitimate monetization methods.

Users must not manipulate advertisements or encourage artificial clicks, impressions or other invalid activity.

Content intended primarily to deceive users or generate artificial advertising activity may be removed.

12. Rewards and Referral Abuse

Community features must not be used to manipulate the Online Quick Tools rewards or referral system.

Users must not create fake accounts, use automated systems, or otherwise attempt to generate artificial rewards.

Suspicious activity may result in rewards being withheld, reversed or accounts being restricted.

13. Moderation

Online Quick Tools may use automated systems, manual review, or a combination of both to identify potentially problematic content.

Not all content can be reviewed immediately.

The presence of content on the platform does not necessarily mean that Online Quick Tools endorses or verifies that content.

14. Content Approval

Some submitted content may require administrator approval before it becomes publicly visible.

Approval may be denied without providing a detailed explanation in every case.

Approved content may later be removed if it is found to violate this policy or create a legal, security or safety concern.

15. Content Removal

We may remove, restrict or disable access to content when we reasonably believe that it:

  • Violates this policy
  • Violates our Terms & Conditions
  • Violates applicable law
  • Infringes another person's rights
  • Creates security or safety risks
  • Contains spam or abuse
  • Creates a significant risk to the platform or its users

16. Account Restrictions

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions on an account.

This may include disabling access to specific community features or other website services.

17. Reporting Content

If you believe that content on Online Quick Tools violates this policy, applicable law, copyright rules, privacy rights or another person's rights, please contact us through the available support or contact method on the website.

When reporting content, provide enough information for us to understand the issue and identify the relevant content.

18. Copyright Complaints

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been used without permission, you may contact us with information identifying the work and the content you believe infringes your rights.

We may review legitimate complaints and take appropriate action where reasonably necessary.

19. False Reports

Do not knowingly submit false or misleading reports against other users or content.

Abuse of the reporting system may itself result in restrictions.

20. No Guarantee of User Content

Online Quick Tools does not guarantee the accuracy, reliability, quality, legality or safety of user-submitted content.

Users should independently evaluate information provided by other users.

21. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Community & Content Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, features, legal requirements or moderation practices.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

22. Related Policies

Please also review:

  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms & Conditions

23. Contact

If you have a question about this policy or need to report content, please use the contact or support options available on Online Quick Tools.

24. Agreement

By submitting or sharing content through Online Quick Tools, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to follow this Community & Content Policy.
